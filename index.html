Everything you need to know before your first strip.

Will I actually feel it?

That’s the point. Because the strip dissolves on your tongue, the actives go to work fast. Energy and Sleep are quick and noticeable; Calm and Multivitamin build over a few weeks, like any good routine.

How do I take one — and how does it taste?

Place one strip on your tongue and let it dissolve for a few seconds. No water, nothing to swallow. They’re lightly flavored and genuinely taste good — clean and subtle, never chalky.

Why a strip instead of a pill or gummy?

Pills and gummies take the long way through digestion. A strip dissolves and absorbs directly — faster, simpler, and far easier to keep up with wherever you are.

How many strips are in a box?

30 strips in every box — about a month of once-a-day use. Two-box duos are 60 strips; the Variety Pack is 120.

Can I combine products?

Check each label and ask a health professional before combining supplements or medications. Bundles are shopping options, not directions to use products together.

What’s actually in them?

Every product page lists the full panel — actives with amounts plus the supporting ingredients that form, flavor and stabilize the strip. Sports Energy carries a labeled 80 mg of natural caffeine; Sleep has no added melatonin.
